Privacy Policy

Khayubdi is currently a Closed Beta fitness application. This page explains how the beta build handles data on your device.

Local-device storage

The current beta stores workout entries, food logs, preferences, draft plans, diagnostics settings, and app state in your browser on the device you use. This helps the app work like a mobile PWA without requiring a production account backend for beta testing.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics exports are designed for troubleshooting. They include app version, build, platform, browser, viewport, language, timezone, PWA status, service worker/cache version, and storage summary only. They should not include personal workout history, nutrition logs, localStorage values, API keys, auth tokens, or private user data.

Beta feedback

Feedback forms in this beta are placeholders unless a tester separately sends feedback through an approved channel. Do not include sensitive personal information in beta feedback.

Fitness guidance

Khayubdi provides fitness and wellness guidance for beta testing. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat health conditions.

Changes during beta

Because this is a Closed Beta, storage behavior, diagnostics, and account features may change before public release. Material changes should be reflected in future beta notes or release pages.
